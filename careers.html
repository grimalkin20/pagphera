Collective & Opportunities

Join the
Pagphera Collective.

We are a tight-knit ensemble of cinematographers, visual artists, and editors devoted to documenting authentic human relationships with artistic rigor and reverence.

How we work

Quiet presence, fierce intentionality.

We do not direct people like film actors, nor do we blast flash into emotional moments. We cultivate deep patience, moving silently within celebrations to capture what is already beautiful, honest, and unrepeatable.

Every member of our crew receives comprehensive creative freedom, masterclass training in color grading and narrative pacing, and equal artistic respect.
