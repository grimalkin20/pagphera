Destination Travel Logistics

How are crew travel fees calculated?

We provide all-inclusive quotes encompassing flights, baggage permits for high-end cinema bodies, local transfers, and crew accommodation. No surprise receipts after the wedding.

When does the crew arrive on-site?

For destination weddings, we always arrive a full 24–48 hours before the first official ceremony. This guarantees time to scout light angles, meet your families, and adjust to local terrain.

Are international permits handled by Pagphera?

Yes. Our production coordinator handles ATA carnets, customs documentation, and commercial equipment insurance worldwide.
