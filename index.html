The Pagphera point of view

A modern approach to an age old tradition

Considered to be among the finest in documentary wedding photography, Pagphera has transformed how Indian weddings are remembered. For years we have created photographs and films which are timeless — etched in memories forever.

We look for the quiet glances, the hands that reach first, the laughter just outside the ceremony. Every story begins months before the wedding day, in conversations about who you are and what matters most.
