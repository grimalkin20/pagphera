Rajasthan · Palace Chapter

A golden afternoon
in Jodhpur.

Mira and Kabir chose the sandstone terraces of Jodhpur for their vows. Across three days, the desert light moved through chhatris and archways, turning every conversation into something painted.

Couple: Mira & Kabir
Location: Umaid Bhawan, Jodhpur
Date: October 2024
Coverage: Photography & Cinematography

There is a specific stillness that descends on a bridal suite about an hour before the baraat arrives. The bustling aunties step into the hallway, the makeup artist makes one last adjustment to the maang tikka, and for three minutes, nobody speaks.

That is the photograph we look for. Not the one where everyone smiles on count of three, but the one where Mira touched her grandmother's hand through the mirror, acknowledging the gravity of what was about to unfold.

Kabir had written his vows on an envelope folded into his sherwani pocket. During the pheras around the sacred fire, as Vedic chants bounced off sandstone jharokhas, you could see his fingers unconsciously tracing the crease of the paper.

These are the quiet gestures that become the spine of an heirloom album. Below are the fragments of their celebration, captured without interruption.

“When Pagphera delivered our photographs, it felt like reliving our wedding through an artist's memories rather than a vendor's checklist. They captured feelings we didn't even realize were visible.”

— Mira & Kabir
