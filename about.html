How we work

Unhurried, present, and always in the room.

We listen first.

Before we photograph anything, we listen — to who is coming, what matters most, and the specific texture of your family and your day. We do not apply a template to your celebration.

We photograph people, not setups.

Direction is available when it helps. Mostly, we work from observation. The best photographs from a wedding come from what actually happened, not from what was arranged.

We return you to the day.

The final gallery and film are not a highlight reel of the best-lit moments. They are a curated sequence of the actual story — including the quiet, the unplanned, and the in-between.
