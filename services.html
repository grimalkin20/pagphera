Before we begin

A few things couples often ask.

Can we combine photography and film?

Yes. Tell us which events you would like covered and we can discuss a coordinated photography and film commission.

Can coverage include several wedding events?

We can plan for a single ceremony or a series of gatherings. Your proposal will set out the dates, hours and team included.

Do you travel for destination weddings?

Destination work is considered by location and availability. Travel and production arrangements are agreed with you before booking.

How do we receive our photographs and films?

Delivery details and the expected schedule are confirmed in your proposal. Printed albums and additional formats can be discussed separately.
