Available services

Commission 01

Editorial Wedding Photography

Documentary and fine art coverage of your complete wedding celebration — from private morning rituals through to midnight festivities. We seek what is real, quiet, and sacred rather than directing poses.

  • Multi-day & full-day coverage by principal directors
  • Comprehensive bridal portraiture and family documentation
  • Private, password-protected online client gallery
  • High-resolution archival master files delivered on custom drive

Commission 02

Cinematic Wedding Storytelling

Films created with the same artistic restraint we bring to still photographs. We capture authentic audio, dialogue, and laughter, composing a cinematic document that mirrors the day’s actual emotional gravity.

  • Director's Cut Highlight Film (5–8 minutes, 4K Cinema)
  • Feature Documentary Film (30–60 minutes full ceremony & speeches)
  • Multi-track high-fidelity spatial audio mastering
  • Color grading crafted in DaVinci Resolve

Commission 03

Pre-Wedding Journals & Films

An unhurried portrait session before the wedding day — at a historic haveli, ancestral home, or destination landscape. A chance to be photographed together without the ticking clock of wedding rituals.

  • Half-day or full-day location portraiture
  • Optional short cinematic prologue film (2–3 minutes)
  • Creative moodboard and architectural location scouting
  • Editorial styling recommendations

Destination Wedding Collections

We accompany couples worldwide. Whether your vows take place along Rajasthan's royal lakes, Goa's coastline, or an Italian villa, our production team handles all international travel logistics seamlessly.

  • All-inclusive multi-day domestic and international coverage
  • Pre-wedding welcome party & rehearsal dinner coverage

  • On-site reconnaissance 24–48 hours prior to ceremonies

Heirloom Handcrafted Albums

Photographs are meant to be touched, turned, and handed down across generations. Our albums are individually curated, printed on museum-grade fine art cotton rag paper, and bound by master bookbinders.

  • Custom linen, Japanese silk, or vegetable-tanned leather bindings
  • Archival pigment printing on 100% cotton rag paper
  • Handcrafted clamshell presentation boxes
  • Parent & family duplicate gift editions
