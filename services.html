What we offer

Coverage for the day you are building.

Every celebration is different. We discuss your wedding, understand what matters most, and design coverage around the actual day.

Prototype preview. Services and descriptions below are illustrative. Final offerings, inclusions, and pricing will be confirmed with the studio before launch.

Available services

01

Wedding photography

Documentary and editorial coverage of your wedding day — from the morning preparations through the late evening celebrations. We document what is real, not what is posed.

  • Full-day or partial-day coverage
  • Curated high-resolution gallery
  • Online gallery delivery
  • Archival digital files
Inquire about photography ↗

02

Wedding cinematography

A wedding film made with the same attention we bring to the photographs. Honest audio, considered pacing, and the movement between the moments — not a music video, but a document of how the day actually felt.

  • Highlight film (four to seven minutes)
  • Full ceremony and speech recordings on request
  • Synchronized audio capture
  • Digital delivery in high resolution
View wedding films ↗

03

Pre-wedding photography & films

A session before the wedding — in a location meaningful to you, or at the venue itself. A way to be photographed together without the ceremony's weight, and a way for us to understand how you move together.

  • Two-to-three-hour photography session
  • Optional short film edit
  • Location guidance and creative direction
  • Curated gallery delivery
Inquire about a pre-wedding session ↗

04

Destination wedding coverage

We travel. Whether the wedding is at a Rajasthan fort, a Goa beach house, or a venue abroad, coverage can be arranged around your date and location. Travel logistics and coverage will be confirmed in conversation.

  • Multi-day coverage available
  • Photography and cinematography combination
  • Travel logistics discussed at inquiry
  • Location scouting where useful
Plan your destination wedding ↗

05

Wedding albums

A curated selection of photographs from your wedding day, designed and printed as a physical heirloom. Album specifications, paper choices, and binding will be confirmed with the studio.

  • Album design and layout curation
  • Print and binding options discussed at inquiry
  • Delivered to your address
Inquire about albums ↗

A note on pricing.

Pricing depends on your wedding's date, location, coverage duration, and specific requirements. We do not publish fixed packages because most weddings need a bespoke conversation. Use the inquiry form to share your details and we'll prepare a proposal based on your actual day.
