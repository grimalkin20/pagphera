Vivah & Traditional Ceremonies

Wedding & Sacred Ritual Photography

Comprehensive documentary fine-art coverage honoring every Vedic ritual: the Solah Shringar, Haldi snan, Kanyadaan, and the Saat Phere around the holy agni. We observe without interference, capturing the sanctity and reverence of both families.

  • Senior lead documentary photographers on-site
  • Full ceremony coverage from dawn preparations to bidaai
  • Heirloom bridal portraits with natural ambient lighting
  • Curated, color-graded high-resolution digital master collection
  • Private, password-protected cloud gallery for lifetime preservation

Cinematic 4K Films & Teaser Reels

We craft wedding cinema with narrative depth, spatial audio, and organic color grading. We capture the tremble in your father's voice during the sehra bandi, the mother's whispered aashirwad, and the rhythm of the shehnai echoing in the courtyard.

  • Feature Wedding Film (25–45 minutes of complete ceremony & vows)
  • Director's Cut Cinematic Highlight Reel (5–8 minutes, 4K Cinema)
  • Curated Vertical Teaser Reels (60–90 seconds) for immediate sharing
  • Multi-channel spatial audio recording with discrete lavalier mics
  • Master color grading in DaVinci Resolve honoring warm Indian skin tones
